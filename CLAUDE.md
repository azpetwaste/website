# Arizona Pet Waste: website and field desk

Read this first. New to the field desk (Joe calls it the Field App)? Read `docs/FIELD_APP.md`. Then read `docs/HANDOFF.md`: two Claude sessions push to this repo, and that file says what the other one changed and what Joe asked for. `README.md` has how the site is built. `GO_LIVE.md` has account setup.

## What is here
- The public site (Cloudflare Pages, boards in `src/boards`, do not hand edit those).
- The server: `src/worker/worker.js`. It answers `/api/*` only. Sign up, Stripe, My Yard, and the field desk.
- The field desk: `static/crew/index.html` (staff), `static/t/index.html` (customer truck link), `supabase/crm.sql` (tables).
- Demo, no sign in: `/crew/?demo` (made up yards, lives in the browser tab only) and `/t/DEMO01` (a made up visit that loops every 3 minutes, served by `trackDemo`).
- Crash log: server errors and crashes in the crew app or on a truck link go to the `errors` table. The office reads them on the Health tab (`/api/office/health`) under "What went wrong". The screen groups the same problem into one card with a count, says where it happened in plain words (`WHERE`), what it means and who fixes it (`plainErr` in the crew page). A new kind of server error should get a line in `plainErr`, or it shows as "Look at this" with the raw words. "Clear the list" saves a time in `settings` (`health_cleared_at`) and the list shows what came after it. It never deletes a row.
- Who runs a route (Joe, Nov 18, 2026: Juan is an owner and he scoops): `staff.scoops`. A scooper always runs a route. An owner or office person does when "Runs a route" is on for them on the Team screen. The week plan and new sign ups only give yards to these people (`runsRoute`, `officeWeek`, `defaultScooper`). With nobody set, the old rule holds: the first person on the team. Do not go back to picking by role alone, or an owner who scoops gets no yards.
- Leads (Joe, Nov 18, 2026): every lead shows the date and time it came in, Arizona time, and a clock. New reads "Waiting 9 hr". The first time a lead leaves New, the server saves `leads.contacted_at` once and the card reads "Reached in 6 min". A note alone does not stop the clock, and a later status never moves the time.
- Staff training page: `static/staff-73d752e4/` (video and steps). Not indexed, not in the sitemap, not linked from any page. Keep it out of `robots.txt` too, since listing it there would show the address.
- One Supabase database for everything. The Stripe webhook keeps `customers` in sync, so every sign up shows up in the office on its own.
