What we send

Service texts

  • A text after you sign up to set your visit day and time
  • A reminder the night before each visit, with a 30 minute window
  • A text when we are on the way
  • A text when we are done, with a link to the photo of your latched gate
  • Changes to your schedule
  • Billing notices
  • Replies to your texts and questions

Deals texts

We do not send deals texts. No form asks you for them, and saying yes to visit texts does not sign you up for any.

Waitlist text

If we are not in your area yet, you can join our waitlist. If you check the text box on that form, we send you one text when we start in your area.

How you opt in

Our sign up form has one box for texts. It is for service texts about your visits. It is not checked for you, so the choice is yours. It is marked Recommended, and you can skip it. If you skip it, we do not text you, and your gate photo comes by email and in My Yard.

The same box is in My Yard, your account page. You can change your choice there any time.

Our waitlist form has one box of its own, for the one text that tells you we started in your area. It is not checked for you either.

If you text us first, we will text you back to answer. Your text counts as your OK for those replies.

Saying yes to texts is not a condition of buying anything from us.

You must be 18 or older to sign up. Only give us a mobile number that is yours. If you change or give up your number, tell us or reply STOP first.

Step by step

  1. Go to arizonapetwaste.com/book/.
  2. Tap your city.
  3. Tap how many dogs you have and how often we should come. Then tap Continue.
  4. Now you are on step 3 of 4, “Tell us about you.” Type your full name, mobile phone, email and street address. Then tap your ZIP code.
  5. Under “Texts from us” you see the one box. It is not checked. Check it to get texts about your visits, or leave it empty.
  6. Tap Continue to go on with your sign up. We keep a record of your choice, with the date and time.

We text you only if you check the box. You can book with the box empty, and then we email you instead.

How often we text

  • Service texts: message frequency varies. Most of the time, you get 2 to 4 texts for each visit.
  • Waitlist text: one text, when we start in your area.

While your service is paused, or when you skip a visit at least 24 hours ahead, we do not send visit reminders.

Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any text to stop all texts from us. We will send one last text to confirm. Replies like QUIT, END, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE also stop texts. STOP does not cancel your plan. To cancel your plan, [[self:use My Yard||call or text 623.309.6104]].

Once you stop all texts, you will no longer get visit reminders or gate photo links by text.

To get texts again, reply START.

Help

Reply HELP to any text, and we will text you back with how to reach us. You can also call or text 623.309.6104, or email info@arizonapetwaste.com.

Your privacy

We never share your mobile number or your text opt in consent with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. We never sell them.

Text opt in data and consent are not shared with any third party, other than the companies that deliver our texts, like our texting provider and your mobile carrier. They may use your number only to deliver our texts.

Our privacy policy explains how we handle your data. Our terms of service cover the rest of our service.
