Who we are

Arizona Pet Waste is a dog poop pickup service in Arizona’s West Valley. In these terms, “we” and “us” mean Arizona Pet Waste. “You” means the person who signs up for our service.

You must be 18 or older to sign up. Our privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Our service

You do not pick your visit day. We set it for you. We run set routes, and that keeps our prices down. After you sign up, we reach out to let you know your day and time.

Need us sooner, like before a party? Text us. We can come the same day for $49, when we have a spot.

On your set day, we pick up all the dog poop in your yard. We bag it and put it in your trash can. On the twice a week plan, you get two set days each week.

At every visit, we spray a free deodorizer on the spots your dogs use most. Keep your dogs off the sprayed spots until they are dry. If you do not want the spray, tell us and we will skip it.

On visit day

  • The night before, we text you a 30 minute window for your visit, if you said yes to service texts.
  • While we are on the way, you can watch our truck on a live map.
  • When we leave, we send you a photo of your shut, latched gate.

Service area

We serve every home in these Arizona ZIP codes:

  • Buckeye, including Verrado (85326 and 85396)
  • Goodyear (85338 and 85395)
  • Litchfield Park (85340)
  • Waddell, including Citrus Park (85355)

We can only serve yards inside these ZIP codes. See the full list on our Areas we serve page. If you are not sure your yard is in our area, call or text us.

Plans and prices

Every plan is billed monthly and covers 1 or 2 dogs.

  • Every week: $108 a month
  • Twice a week: $189 a month
  • Every other week: $75 a month

Each dog after two adds $13 a month. On the every other week plan, each dog after two adds $9 a month. For example, 3 dogs on the every week plan cost $121 a month. On the every other week plan, 3 dogs cost $84 a month.

Extras

  • Turf odor treatment: $35 each time
  • One time cleanup: $95

To add an extra, ask us. We charge for an extra only when you ask for it. Extras go on your next bill on the 1st.

First month free

You add a card when you sign up, and we do not charge it that day. Your free month includes every visit on your plan.

Your free month runs one month and one week from the soonest day we can start. The extra week gives us time to reach out and set your first visit. The sign up page shows your dates before you add your card.

Black Friday deal: sign up by Monday, November 30, 2026, and your first month is free.

Your free month stays free when you keep your plan for 60 days. The 60 days start on the first day of your free month. If you ask to cancel in those 60 days, we charge one month at your plan price, minus anything you have paid us for your plan. We show you the exact date at checkout, before you save your card.

The free month is one per home. If your home or phone number has had a plan with us before, call or text us to start again.

Billing

We charge everyone on the 1st of each month. Each charge pays for the month ahead. We charge your card each month until you cancel.

Your first charge is on the 1st of the month your free month ends. It only covers the days after your free month, so it is a smaller, fair amount. For example, if your free month runs until December 27, on December 1 we charge for December 27 to January 1. On January 1, we charge your full monthly price.

Stripe handles our payments. We never see or store your full card number.

If a payment fails, we will try it again. We may pause your visits until the payment goes through.

Skips and pauses

You get 4 free skips a year. To skip a visit, tell us at least 24 hours before it.

After your first 60 days, you can also pause your service for up to 120 days in My Yard. You do not pay while your service is paused.

You will not get visit reminders while your service is paused. The same goes for a visit you skip at least 24 hours ahead.

Canceling

There is no contract. You can cancel any time in My Yard, and you do not need to call us.

If you cancel in your first 60 days, we charge one month at your plan price, minus anything you have paid, as the First month free section says.

After you cancel, your visits keep going until the end of the month you already paid for. Then they stop, and we do not charge you again. For example, if you cancel on the 20th, your visits keep going until the end of that month.

We do not give refunds for part of a month. When you cancel, we delete your gate code.

Price changes

We may change our prices. If we do, we will tell you by email or text at least 30 days before the new price starts. If you do not want to pay the new price, you can cancel before it starts.

Your yard and dogs

  • Keep any dog that might bite inside on visit day, or tell us about it first.
  • If a dog needs to come inside, we will text you and ask.
  • Tell us about anything in your yard that is not safe for us.
  • Tell us if your number of dogs changes, since it can change your price.

Gate access and photos

You give us your gate code or notes on how to get into your yard. We use them only for your visits. If your gate code changes, tell us before your next visit. We delete your gate code when you cancel.

When we leave, we shut and latch your gate. Then we send you a photo of it, so you can see how we left it. We keep gate photos for 90 days.

If we cannot get in safely, we will text you and try again at the next chance we get. This can happen if your gate is locked, a dog is loose or there is a hazard. We do not refund a visit we could not do because we could not get in.

Weather and holidays

Extreme heat, monsoon storms or holidays can move your visit by up to 48 hours. If that happens, we will text you.

The “Something’s off” promise

If something is not right after a visit, tell us within 24 hours. Tap “Something’s off” in My Yard, or text us at 623.309.6104. We will come back for free within 24 hours.

Damage and liability

If you think we damaged something, tell us within 48 hours of the visit. You can email, call or text us. Tell us what happened, and send a photo if you can.

Our liability to you for any claim is limited to what you paid us in the last month. This limit applies as far as Arizona law allows.
