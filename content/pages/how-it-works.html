Here is what happens from the day you sign up to the photo of your latched gate. Same steps, every visit.

1. Sign up in about 2 minutes.

Type your ZIP, pick your plan and add your card. You pay $0 today. Your first month is free, and there is no contract.

2. We set your day.

You do not pick a day. We run set routes, and that keeps our prices down. After you sign up, we reach out to let you know your day and time.

3. A text the night before.

The night before each visit, we text you a 30 minute window. No texts? We email you instead.

4. Watch us come.

On your day, tap the link in your text to see our truck on a live map. No app needed.

5. In your yard.

  • We shut the gate behind us first.
  • We pick up every pile, front to back, in rows.
  • We rake rock yards, so nothing hides.
  • We spray free sanitizer and deodorizer on the spots your dogs use most.
  • We bag the poop and put it in your trash can.

6. A photo of your latched gate.

When we leave, we latch the gate, pull on it, and send you a photo. If a visit has no gate photo, you pay $0 for that visit.

7. Missed a pile? We come back free.

Text us and we come back for free within 24 hours.

Clean tools at every yard.

We clean our boots and tools before every yard. That keeps germs from going from yard to yard.
