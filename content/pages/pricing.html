How billing works

Black Friday sale: sign up by Monday, November 30, and your first month is free. The sale ends at midnight. Keep your plan for 60 days and your free month stays free. If you cancel in your first 60 days, we charge one month at your plan price, minus anything you have paid.

We charge everyone on the 1st of each month. With the sale, your first month is free. Your first charge only covers the days after your free month, so it is smaller. Then you pay the full price on the 1st of each month.

How to compare prices

  • Ask if the price is per visit or per month.
  • Ask if there is a sign up fee or a first cleanup fee.
  • Ask if the spray costs extra.
  • Ask if you get a photo after every visit.
  • Ask if they haul the poop away or leave it in your trash can.
