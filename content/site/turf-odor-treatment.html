Why turf smells

Dog pee runs through the turf blades. It soaks into the backing and the infill under it. Then the sun heats it up, and the smell comes back every afternoon.

What you get for free

Every visit comes with a free deodorizer spray on the spots your dogs use most. For most yards, that is enough.

When to add the deeper treatment

  • The turf still smells after a visit.
  • It is summer and the smell is strong by afternoon.
  • You have two or more dogs on a small patch of turf.
  • You have guests coming over.

What you can do between visits

  • Rinse the turf with a hose once a week. Rinse more in summer.
  • Brush the turf so water gets down to the infill.
