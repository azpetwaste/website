Ask nine questions before you hire a dog poop pickup service. The answers tell you the real price, how safe your dogs are and how easy it is to leave.

The nine questions

1. Do you come to my town?

Check that the company lists your town or ZIP code on its site. Not every West Valley company goes to every town.

2. What is the full price for my dogs?

A low price in an ad is often for one dog. Ask for the price with all of your dogs, and ask if it is per visit or per month. See what a pooper scooper service costs in Arizona.

3. Is there a sign up fee or a first cleanup fee?

Some companies charge more for the first visit if the yard has not been cleaned in a while. Ask before you book.

4. Are you licensed and insured?

A worker will be in your yard, near your gate and your dogs. Ask if the company is licensed and insured.

5. How do I know the gate is shut?

An open gate is how dogs get lost. Ask if you get a photo of the latched gate after every visit.

6. Will I know when you are coming?

Ask if you get a text before the visit, and how big the time window is.

7. Where does the waste go?

Some companies haul it away. Some bag it and put it in your trash can. Ask so there are no surprises.

8. Do you clean your tools between yards?

Germs and worm eggs can ride on boots and tools from one yard to the next. Ask if tools get cleaned after each yard.

9. How do I skip, pause or cancel?

Ask if there is a contract. Ask if you can cancel online or if you have to call.
