Pick up dog poop at least once a week. Twice a week is better in the Arizona summer, with two or more dogs, or when kids play in the yard.

Why a week is the limit.

Flies lay eggs in fresh poop. UC pest experts say house flies can go from egg to adult in as little as 7 days in summer. They also say removing dog poop at least once a week cuts down on flies. Wait longer than a week and a new batch of flies hatches in your yard.

The math.

A dog that goes twice a day leaves 14 piles a week. Two dogs leave 28. Skip a week with two dogs and you are looking for 56 piles.

What Arizona heat changes.

Dry heat bakes a pile hard in a day or two. A dry pile is harder to see on rock and turf, and it does not break down. It just sits. Then a monsoon storm soaks the old piles and spreads them across the yard. See how long dog poop takes to decompose.

Smell also builds faster in the heat. Read how to get rid of dog poop smell in your yard.

What Goodyear city code says.

The City of Goodyear says pets cannot cause flies, bugs or smell, and that manure or droppings must be removed from the property at least twice a week. If you live in Goodyear, twice a week is the rule to follow. See the rules for each town in dog poop laws in the West Valley.

Why it matters for your family.

The EPA says it is a good idea to pick up after your dog at home, too. Some diseases can pass from pet waste to people through the soil, and kids who play in the yard are most at risk. Read more in can dog poop make you sick.

Make it easy.

  • Pick the same day each week so you do not forget.
  • Walk the yard in rows so you cover every foot of it.
  • Go early in the morning in summer.
  • Tie the bag shut and put it in the trash can.
