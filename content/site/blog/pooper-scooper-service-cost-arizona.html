What changes the price

  • Number of dogs. Many companies charge more for each dog. Ask what the price is for your number of dogs.
  • How often. Weekly visits cost less per visit than every other week, since there is less to pick up each time.
  • First cleanup. Some companies charge extra for the first visit if the yard has not been cleaned in a while.
  • Yard size. Some companies charge more for large lots.
  • Extras. Deodorizer spray, turf treatment and same day visits can cost extra.

Per visit or per month?

Some companies show a price per visit. Others show a price per month. To compare, turn a weekly price into a monthly one. There are about 4.3 weeks in a month, so $20 a week is about $87 a month.

Questions to ask before you sign up

  • Is the price per visit or per month?
  • Is that price for one dog or for all of my dogs?
  • Is there a sign up fee or a first cleanup fee?
  • Does deodorizer spray cost extra?
  • Do I get a photo after every visit?
  • Where does the waste go?
  • Is there a contract, and what does it cost to cancel?
  • Are you licensed and insured?

Is it worth it?

It comes down to your time. Scooping is a chore you have to do every week, and in an Arizona summer you do it in heat over 100 degrees. If you would pay about $25 a week to skip it, a service makes sense. If you keep up with it on your own, you do not need one.
