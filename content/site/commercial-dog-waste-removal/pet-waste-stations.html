How often stations need service.

Most stations need one visit a week. A busy station by a dog park, a trailhead or an apartment building can fill up in three or four days, so it gets two visits a week.

Summer changes things. A full can in 110 degree heat smells fast and draws flies. Keep the schedule tight from May through September.

Where to put stations.

Put stations where walks start and where dogs stop. Good spots:

  • Entrances to the community and to each trail
  • Next to dog parks and greenbelts
  • By mailbox clusters
  • Near apartment building doors and pet relief areas
  • Along long walking paths

Keep each station right next to a path so people walk past it. A station tucked behind a wall or a bush gets skipped.

Stations in the Arizona sun.

Our sun is hard on stations. Plastic signs fade and crack. Bags left in a dispenser in full sun can turn brittle. Cans heat up, and heat makes smell worse.

When you buy new stations, look for metal or thick plastic parts, a lid that closes on its own and a sign made to hold its color in the sun. Shade from a tree or a wall helps when you can get it.
