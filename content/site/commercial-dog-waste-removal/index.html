Why dog waste needs a plan.

Dog waste can carry roundworms, hookworms and germs like E. coli and Salmonella. It stays in the grass long after it dries out. When monsoon storms hit, rain washes it into washes and storm drains.

Smell, flies and piles near walkways also lead to complaints from residents, tenants and neighbors. A set schedule keeps the problem from building up between complaints.

Local rules.

  • Buckeye: city code says animal waste cannot be left in a way that is not clean, on public land or private land.
  • Goodyear: city code says droppings must be removed from yards at least twice a week, and pets cannot cause flies or smell.
  • Litchfield Park: city code says dog waste must be picked up right away in parks and on public land, and it goes in a closed bag in the trash.
  • Citrus Park and Waddell: the county health code says animal waste must be handled so it does not breed insects.

Most HOAs add their own pickup rules on top of these.
