The twice a week rule.

Goodyear city code says droppings must be removed from yards at least twice a week, and pets cannot cause flies, smell or piles of droppings. The rule is written for yards, and it makes a good standard for busy common areas and dog parks too.

For most Goodyear HOAs, that means two visits a week at the dog parks and busiest stations, and one visit a week everywhere else.

Dog parks and 55 plus communities.

Goodyear has public dog parks at Roscoe Dog Park on W Roeser Road and at Goodyear Community Park, at Litchfield Road and Thomas Road. PebbleCreek and CantaMia have dog parks just for residents.

Resident dog parks get used every day. We scoop the whole park, empty the cans, refill the bags and wipe down the stations, early in the morning in summer.

Apartments, offices and shops.

Goodyear has a growing mix of apartments, offices and shops along Interstate 10, from Palm Valley to Estrella Parkway. Pet friendly properties need relief areas that stay clean. We service relief areas, dog runs and stations, and we can come before your busiest hours.

Spring training and events.

Every spring, baseball fans come to Goodyear for spring training, and dog friendly festivals and runs fill the calendar too. We bring loaner stations, stay on site during the event and sweep the grounds after.

A year in Goodyear common areas.

  • October to April: peak walking season. PebbleCreek, CantaMia and other 55 plus communities get busier with winter residents, and spring training brings crowds in February and March.
  • May and June: the heat pushes walks to dawn and after dark, so waste builds up on the same few paths. We come early in the morning.
  • July to September: monsoon storms can wash waste into drains and lakes, and wet waste smells worse in the heat.

Lakes, trails and greenbelts.

Estrella has two private lakes and miles of trails. Palm Valley and Canyon Trails have long paved paths. Residents notice waste near water, playgrounds and paths first.

We scoop paths and greenbelts in a pattern so nothing gets missed, and we check the edges near lakes and play areas on every visit.
