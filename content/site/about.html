How we work.

  • You always know. We text you the night before with a 30 minute window. On your day, you can watch our truck on a live map.
  • Proof every visit. We latch your gate, pull on it, and send you a photo. No gate photo means you pay $0 for that visit.
  • A fresh yard. Free deodorizer spray at every visit.
  • Clean tools. We clean our boots and tools after every yard, so germs do not go from yard to yard.
  • You are in charge. Skip, pause, see your bills or cancel in My Yard. No phone call.

Licensed, bonded and insured.

Arizona Pet Waste is licensed, bonded and insured. Your home, your gate and your dogs are covered while we are in your yard.
