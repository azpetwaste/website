The twice a week rule

Goodyear city code says droppings must be removed from yards at least twice a week, and pets cannot cause flies, smell or piles of droppings.

The rule is written for yards, and it makes a good standard for busy common areas too. For most Goodyear HOAs, that means two visits a week at the dog parks and busiest stations, and one visit a week everywhere else.

Dog parks and 55 plus communities

Goodyear has public dog parks at Roscoe Dog Park on W Roeser Road and at Goodyear Community Park. PebbleCreek and CantaMia have dog parks just for residents.

Resident dog parks get used every day. We scoop the whole park, empty the cans, refill the bags and wipe down the stations, early in the morning in summer. See dog park cleaning.

Apartments, offices and shops

Goodyear has a growing mix of apartments, offices and shops along Interstate 10, from Palm Valley to Estrella Parkway. Pet friendly properties need relief areas that stay clean.

We service relief areas, dog runs and stations, and we can come before your busiest hours. See apartment pet waste management.

Spring training and events

Every spring, baseball fans come to Goodyear for spring training, and dog friendly festivals and runs fill the calendar too.

We bring loaner stations, stay on site during the event and sweep the grounds after. See event pet waste cleanup.

A year of dog waste in Goodyear

@@clock@@

October to April

Peak walking season. PebbleCreek, CantaMia and other 55 plus communities fill up with winter residents, and spring training brings crowds in February and March.

@@sun@@

May and June

The heat arrives. People walk dogs at dawn and after dark, so waste piles up on the same few paths. We come early in the morning.

@@rain@@

July to September

Monsoon season. One storm can wash a week of waste into washes and drains, and wet waste smells worse in the heat. Keep the schedule tight.

Lakes, trails and greenbelts

Estrella has two private lakes and miles of trails. Palm Valley and Canyon Trails have long paved paths. Residents notice waste near water, playgrounds and paths first.

We scoop paths and greenbelts in a pattern so nothing gets missed, and we check the edges near lakes and play areas on every visit.

What goes into a Goodyear quote

Goodyear stretches from north of Interstate 10 down to Estrella, south of the Gila River. A Palm Valley site sits close to our routes. Estrella takes a longer drive.

Your quote counts the stations, the minutes it takes to scoop each common area, how often we come and the drive. Bags and liners are included. Plans are month to month and billed on the 1st.
