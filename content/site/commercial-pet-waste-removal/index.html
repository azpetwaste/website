Schedule

How often should dog waste be picked up?

Most HOA common areas need one visit a week. Dog parks, apartment relief areas and busy stations need two. From May to September, heat makes smell and flies worse, so keep the schedule tight.

Why it matters

Why dog waste needs a plan.

@@germ@@

It carries germs

Dog waste can carry roundworms, hookworms, E. coli and Salmonella. It stays in grass and rock long after it dries.

@@rain@@

Monsoon storms wash it away

One storm can carry a week of waste into washes and storm drains. Storm drain water is not treated.

@@chat@@

Residents complain

Smell, flies and piles by the paths turn into calls to the board. A set schedule stops the buildup between complaints.
