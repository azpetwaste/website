HOAs

HOA common areas in Buckeye.

Buckeye HOAs take care of miles of greenbelts and trails. Dog waste builds up fastest at trailheads, park edges, mailbox clusters and the turf near pools and clubhouses.

We scoop those spots on a set day, service every station and send the board a photo report. Many Buckeye parks already have stations. We keep them stocked and emptied before they overflow. See HOA pet waste removal.

Dog parks

Dog parks in Buckeye.

Verrado's Granite Bluff and Marketside dog parks are for residents, and HOA dog parks like them see dogs every day. We scoop the whole park, empty the cans, refill the bags and wipe down the stations.

Most dog parks need scooping once or twice a week. In summer we come early in the morning, before the heat sets in. See dog park cleaning.

Apartments

Apartments and build to rent in Buckeye.

New apartment and build to rent communities keep opening in Buckeye, many near Interstate 10. Most allow dogs, and the pet relief areas fill up fast.

We keep dog runs and relief areas clean and the stations stocked, so the grounds look good when a renter tours. See apartment pet waste management.

Seasons

A year of dog waste in Buckeye.

@@clock@@

October to April

The best walking weather of the year. Trails stay busy, and 55 plus communities fill up with winter residents. Many HOAs add a second weekly visit.

@@sun@@

May and June

The heat arrives. People walk dogs at dawn and after dark, so waste piles up on the same few paths. We come early in the morning.

@@rain@@

July to September

Monsoon season. One storm can wash a week of waste into washes and drains, and wet waste smells worse in the heat. Keep the schedule tight.

Ground

Rock, turf and grass.

Most Buckeye common areas mix desert rock, artificial turf and some grass. On rock, dry waste blends in and gets missed. On turf, urine soaks into the backing and smells in the heat. On grass, waste kills the spot it sits on.

We walk each area in a pattern so nothing gets skipped. We can also add deodorizer spray on turf relief areas.

Pricing

What goes into a Buckeye quote.

Buckeye covers a lot of ground, so distance matters. A site near Interstate 10 sits close to our routes. Sun City Festival and Festival Foothills are about 17 miles north of Interstate 10, so they take more drive time.

Your quote counts the stations, the minutes it takes to scoop each common area, how often we come and the drive. Bags and liners are included. Plans are month to month and billed on the 1st.
