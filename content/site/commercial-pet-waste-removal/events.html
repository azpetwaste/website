How event cleanup works

  1. 1

    Tell us about it

    Date, address, hours and how many people and dogs you expect.

  2. 2

    We plan the stations

    We pick how many stations you need and where, based on your crowd and grounds.

  3. 3

    On event day

    We set up the stations, stay on site and keep them stocked and emptied.

  4. 4

    When it ends

    We sweep the grounds, take down the stations and bag the waste for your dumpster.

Plan your event for the heat

Heat changes when dogs come out and how fast waste smells.

From May to September, morning and evening events work best for dogs and for cleanup. Put stations in the shade when you can, and near water and food areas, where people stop.
