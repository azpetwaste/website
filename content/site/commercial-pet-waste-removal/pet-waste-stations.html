How often should a pet waste station be emptied?

Most stations need one visit a week. A busy station by a dog park, a trailhead or an apartment building can fill up in three or four days, so it gets two.

Station spotVisits a weekWhy
Quiet street or greenbelt1Light, steady use.
Trailhead or dog park2Fills up in 3 or 4 days.
Apartment building2 or 3Many dogs, every day.

A full can in 110 degree heat smells fast and draws flies. Keep the schedule tight from May through September.

Where to put pet waste stations

Put stations where walks start and where dogs stop.

  • Entrances to the community and to each trail
  • Next to dog parks and greenbelts
  • By mailbox clusters
  • Near apartment building doors and pet relief areas
  • Along long walking paths

Keep each station right next to a path so people walk past it. A station tucked behind a wall or a bush gets skipped.

Stations in the Arizona sun

Our sun is hard on stations.

@@sun@@

Signs fade

Plastic signs fade and crack. Pick signs made to hold their color in the sun.

@@bag@@

Bags get brittle

Bags left in a dispenser in full sun can turn brittle. We restock often so they stay fresh.

@@station@@

Cans heat up

Heat makes smell worse. Pick a lid that closes on its own, and use shade from a tree or wall when you can.
