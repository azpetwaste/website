Local fact

No dog park in town.

Litchfield Park does not have its own dog park. That puts more dogs on sidewalks, greenbelts and HOA common areas, and those spots need regular pickup.

A station by each walking path, with a full roll of bags, helps a lot. See pet waste station service.

Condos

Condos and small common areas.

Many Litchfield Park homes share small common areas. A few dogs on a small patch of grass add up fast, and smell hangs around in the heat.

We scoop shared grass and rock areas, service the stations and send a photo report to your board or manager.

Resorts

Resorts and golf communities.

Pet friendly rooms and golf course homes bring dogs onto lawns, paths and turf. We scoop those areas and keep stations stocked, early in the day before guests are out.

Ground

Shade trees and grass.

Litchfield Park has big old trees, golf course lawns and shady grass areas. Grass hides waste in the shade and under leaves, and waste kills the grass it sits on.

We walk grass areas in a pattern and check under trees and along hedges, where waste gets missed most.

Pricing

What goes into a Litchfield Park quote.

Litchfield Park is small and sits close to our Goodyear routes, so drive time stays low.

Your quote counts the stations, the minutes it takes to scoop each common area and how often we come. Bags and liners are included. Plans are month to month and billed on the 1st.
