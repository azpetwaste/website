How often should a dog park be cleaned?

Most dog parks need one or two visits a week. Busy parks need two. Plan an extra visit after a big event or a holiday weekend.

ParkVisits a weekWhy
Small HOA dog park1A handful of regulars.
Busy community dog park2Dogs all day, every day.
Apartment dog run2 or 3Many dogs in a small space.

We time your park on the free site walk and tell you what it needs.

Heat, monsoon and dog parks

Summer changes how a dog park gets used and how fast it smells.

@@sun@@

Early mornings

Dogs come at dawn and after dark in summer. We scoop early, before the heat sets in.

@@rain@@

Monsoon storms

One storm can wash a week of waste across the park and into drains. Keep the schedule tight from July to September.

@@clock@@

Fall and winter

October to April is the busiest walking season, and many parks need a second weekly visit.

Turf, rock and grass

Every kind of ground hides waste in its own way.

Artificial turf

Urine soaks into the turf backing and smells in the heat. We scoop every visit and can add deodorizer spray.

Rock and decomposed granite

Dry waste blends right in. We walk the park in a pattern so nothing gets missed.

Grass

Waste kills the grass it sits on. Quick pickup keeps dead spots from spreading.

Dog parks in our area

We clean HOA, apartment and private dog parks. City parks are cleaned by each city.

In Buckeye, Verrado's Granite Bluff and Marketside dog parks are for residents. In Goodyear, PebbleCreek and CantaMia have dog parks just for residents, and the city runs Roscoe Dog Park and the dog park at Goodyear Community Park.

Litchfield Park does not have its own dog park, so HOA greenbelts there see more dogs. In Waddell, newer communities like Zanjero Pass open parks and trails as each phase opens.
