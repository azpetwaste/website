County rules

County rules.

Citrus Park and Waddell sit outside city limits, so Maricopa County rules apply. The county health code treats animal waste as trash that must be handled so it does not breed insects.

Dogs must be fenced in or on a leash 6 feet long or shorter. HOAs here usually add their own pickup rules.

New homes

New home communities.

New communities in Waddell open parks and trails before every lot is sold. Builders and new HOAs can start station service on day one and add stations as each phase opens.

That way dog waste never gets ahead of you. See pet waste station service.

Kennels

Kennels, boarding and ranch properties.

Parts of Waddell, like Clearwater Farms, have one to two acre lots, some with horse corrals. Kennels, boarding yards and big properties with many dogs need more than a weekly pass.

We scoop runs and relief yards as often as you need.

Seasons

A year of dog waste in Citrus Park and Waddell.

@@clock@@

October to April

Cool weather brings walkers out to greenbelts, parks and trails close to the White Tank Mountains.

@@sun@@

May and June

The heat arrives. People walk dogs at dawn and after dark, so waste piles up on the same few paths. We come early in the morning.

@@rain@@

July to September

Monsoon season. One storm can wash a week of waste into washes and drains, and wet waste smells worse in the heat. Keep the schedule tight.

Ground

Big lots and open space.

Waddell lots run bigger than most in the West Valley, and many HOA greenbelts are wide and open. More ground takes more time to scoop.

We time each area on the free site walk and price it to match.
