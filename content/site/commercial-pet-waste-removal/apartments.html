Clean grounds help on tours

A renter sees the grounds before the unit. Piles by the walkway or a smelly dog run can end a tour early.

We set your visit days around your busiest tour days. Most properties pick a visit just before the weekend, plus one or two during the week.

Your photo report shows the grounds were clean that day. Share it with your owner or regional manager.

What every visit includes

The same checklist at every property, every time.

Turf relief areas in the heat

Many communities use artificial turf for pet areas. Urine soaks into the turf backing and smells in the heat.

We scoop every visit and can add deodorizer spray on turf relief areas. In summer we come early, before the heat bakes in the smell.

How often should apartments pick up dog waste?

Most apartment communities need two or three visits a week. A relief area that many dogs share can fill up in a day or two.
