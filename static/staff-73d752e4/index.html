Get the app

You do this one time. There is nothing to download.

  1. Open arizonapetwaste.com/crew on your phone.iPhone: use Safari. Android: use Chrome.
  2. Type your work email. Tap Send code.The owner adds your email first.
  3. Type the 6 digit code from your email.
  4. Add it to your home screen.iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: menu, then Add to Home screen.
Scooper

Every route

  1. Open the app. Tap Start route.The first time, tap OK and Allow so we can share your location while the route is live.
  2. Keep the app open and on screen while you drive.If you lock the phone or switch apps, the customer's map stops moving.
  3. Tap Send on the orange text bar.It opens Messages with the text written. Hit send. This is how the customer gets the truck link.
  4. Tap Navigate. Drive to the yard.The screen shows the gate code, the dogs and any notes.
  5. Tap Arrived.The app does this for you when you are close.
  6. Scoop every pile. Spray. Latch the gate and pull it.Tap each box as you go.
  7. Tap Gate photo. Take a picture of the latched gate.
  8. Tap Done.The next yard loads by itself.
  9. After the last yard, tap End route.Location sharing turns off.
Office

Each day

  1. New sign up: set their service day.Today tab, under Needs a service day. Then call or text them their day.
  2. Night before: tap Queue tomorrow's texts.Then open the Texts tab and tap Send on each one.
  3. Visit day: watch the Today tab.Each yard shows Scheduled, On the way, Here or Done.
  4. Send any text still waiting in the Texts tab.Use the business phone, 623.309.6104.
  5. End of day: check the red tags.No photo and Can't service both need a call to the customer.
Help

If something goes wrong

Customer says the truck is not movingOpen the app and keep it on screen.
Customer did not get a linkThe text is still waiting. Tap Send. Or copy their link from the Link button on the Today tab.
No code emailCheck spam. Tap Send code again.
You can not do the yardTap Can't service. Pick the reason. Take a photo of the locked gate if you can.
The camera will not workTap No photo. The office gets a flag.
The app shows an errorKeep going. Tell the office. It is saved for us to fix.
