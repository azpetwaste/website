Get the app

You do this one time. There is nothing to download.

  1. Open arizonapetwaste.com/crew on your phone.iPhone: use Safari. Android: use Chrome.
  2. Type your work email. Tap Email me a code.The owner adds your email first.
  3. Type the 6 digit code from your email.
  4. Add it to your home screen.iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: menu, then Add to Home screen.
Scooper

Every route

  1. Open the app. Tap Start route.The first time, tap OK and Allow so we can share your location while the route is live.
  2. Keep the app open and on screen while you drive.If you lock the phone or switch apps, the customer's map stops moving.
  3. Tap the orange bar at the top. It says Send text.It opens Messages with the text written. Hit send. This is how the customer gets the truck link.
  4. Step 1, Drive. Tap Directions and drive to the yard.The screen shows the gate code, the dogs and any notes.
  5. Tap I'm here when you park.The app does this for you when you are close.
  6. Step 2, Scoop. Scoop every pile. Spray. Close and latch the gate.Tap each box when it is done.
  7. Step 3, Photo. Tap Take gate photo. Take one picture of the closed gate.The app writes the date and time on the photo. The photo is our proof we were there. No photo means the visit is free for the customer.
  8. Camera or app not working? Use your phone's camera.Take the picture. Tap Photo trouble, then Upload a photo from this phone. Check the time. It fills in the time from the photo. If you change it, the photo says the time was entered by staff. Always tell the truth about the time.
  9. Wait for the next yard to open.When the photo saves, the yard is done and the next yard loads by itself. If you see Photo not sent, tap Send photo again. The picture is safe on your phone.
  10. After the last yard, tap End my route.Location sharing turns off.
Office

Each day

  1. New sign up: set their service day.Schedule tab, under New sign ups with no day yet. Tap one and pick the day. Then call or text them their day.
  2. Night before: tap Get tomorrow's texts ready.Then open the Texts tab and tap Send on each one.
  3. Visit day: watch the Schedule tab.A yard with no tag is still to do. The rest show On the way, At the yard, Done or Not done.
  4. Send any text still waiting in the Texts tab.Use the business phone, 623.309.6104.
  5. End of day: check the red tags.No photo and Not done both need a call to the customer.
Help

If something goes wrong

Customer says the truck is not movingOpen the app and keep it on screen.
Customer did not get a linkThe text is still waiting. Tap Send. Or tap the yard on the Schedule tab and tap Copy the customer's link.
No code emailCheck spam. Tap Email me a code again.
You can not do the yardTap I can't do this yard. Pick the reason. Tap Tell the office.
The camera will not workTake the photo with your phone's own camera. Tap Photo trouble, then Upload a photo from this phone. Only if you have no photo at all, tap I have no photo at all. That visit is free for the customer.
The app shows an errorKeep going. Tell the office. It is saved for us to fix.
