# Waddell, AZ: what the town page says and where each fact comes from

Checked November 16, 2026, for `/pooper-scooper/waddell-az/` (`design/generator/cities.py` and `longform.py`, area `waddell`).
Rule from Joe: no made up facts about Waddell. Every line on the page that states a fact is listed here with its source.
If a fact changes, fix the page and this file in the same change.

## What Waddell is
| The page says | Source |
|---|---|
| Waddell is a place name and a mailing address, not a city. ZIP 85355. | Wikipedia, Waddell, Arizona: "an unincorporated community in Maricopa County", ZIP 85355. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waddell,_Arizona |
| It has had its own post office since 1937 and never became a city. | Same page: a post office was set up in 1937, and "the town never developed, but it remained a post office and place name." |
| Most homes sit on county land. | Granite Vista is "in an unincorporated area of Waddell" (Your Valley, May 10, 2022). White Tank Foothills is a "county island" approved by the county Board of Supervisors (Your Valley, Sept 30, 2022). Twelve Oaks Estates is "a single family subdivision within Maricopa County" (Glendale annexation narrative, Feb 2025). |
| Some homes, like the ones in Zanjero Pass, are inside the City of Glendale. | Zanjero Pass PRD narrative filed with the City of Glendale, April 2021: "This project site is located within the City of Glendale; however, it is surrounded by Maricopa County jurisdiction." https://public.destinyhosted.com/glenddocs/2021/PC/20210617_3873/5980_Zanjero_Pass_-_PRD_Narrative_Stamped.pdf |
| Waddell sits at the foot of the White Tank Mountains. | The park's address is in Waddell (below). Elliott Homes places Granite Vista "at the edge of the White Tank Mountains". |

Only Zanjero Pass was confirmed as inside Glendale. Do not name another neighborhood as inside a city without a source.
The old town site (Waddell Road and Cotton Lane, 33.611 N, 112.427 W) is north of the part of the map we draw.

## Rules
| The page says | Source |
|---|---|
| On county land, dogs must be kept in on your own land or on a leash 6 feet long or shorter. | Maricopa County Ordinance P 13, section 7. It applies only to unincorporated areas (section 14). https://www.maricopa.gov/6520/ORDINANCE-P-13 |
| The county health code treats animal waste as trash that must be handled so it does not breed insects. We did not find a county rule on how often to scoop a yard. | Same wording as the Citrus Park page and the dog poop laws blog post, checked November 2026. |
| Inside Glendale, city code says animal waste must be cleaned out of yards at least twice a week. | City of Glendale dog waste flyer, which quotes City Code section 25 24: waste "shall be removed from pens, kennels, stables, yards and other enclosures at least twice weekly and from the property at least once each week." https://www.glendaleaz.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/city-clerk/documents/environmental-protection/new-logocall-of-doodie-door-hanger-eng_span.pdf |
| Cortessa, Stonebridge Manor and Windrose have an HOA. | Listings show association fees for Cortessa and Stonebridge Manor. Sentiero is in the Windrose at Zanjero Trails Community Association (David Weekley Homes). |

## White Tank Mountain Regional Park
| The page says | Source |
|---|---|
| 20304 W White Tank Mountain Road, Waddell. Nearly 30,000 acres, the biggest regional park in Maricopa County. Hours: May to October 5 a.m. to 9 p.m., November to April 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. | Maricopa County Parks. https://www.maricopacountyparks.net/park-locator/white-tank-mountain-regional-park/ |
| It is off Olive Avenue. | Maricopa County Library District: the White Tank Library is "off of Olive Ave in Waddell next to the entrance" of the park. https://mcldaz.org/white-tank/ |
| About 30 miles of trails. The first half mile of the Waterfall Trail has a hard, smooth surface. | Park hiking page: "approximately 30 miles of shared use trails", Waterfall Trail "hard surfaced, barrier free" for about half a mile. https://www.maricopacountyparks.net/park-locator/white-tank-mountain-regional-park/park-activities/hiking/ |
| Dogs on a leash 6 feet long or shorter. You must pick up after your dog. | County park rule R 105 (leash not over 6 feet, owners must remove and dispose of waste). https://www.maricopacountyparks.net/park-locator/maricopa-trail/park-rules-maricopa-trail |
| Five trails good for dogs: Black Rock Loop, Gray Fox, South, Waddell (easy, 2 miles out and back) and Waterfall (1.8 miles out and back). Dog water fountains. Use the trailhead trash cans. Hot ground burns paws. | Maricopa County Bark Ranger page. https://www.maricopa.gov/6685 |
