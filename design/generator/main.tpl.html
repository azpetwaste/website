How our dog poop pickup works

Here is what we do every time we come. Six easy steps.

@@SCENE@@
@@SCENE_CTL@@

  1. We text you the night before

    Hi Sam! We will scoop your yard tomorrow between 10:00 and 10:30 AM.

    A text from us

  2. You can watch us drive to you

    Tap the link in your text. You see our truck on a map. No app needed.

  3. We pick up every pile and haul it away

    We shut the gate behind us. We walk in rows, front to back, and pick up every pile. Rock yards get raked. We bag the poop and take it with us.

  4. We spray for free

    We spray deodorizer on the spots your dogs use most. It is free at every visit.

  5. We latch your gate and take a photo

    We latch the gate and pull on it to be sure. Then we take a photo.
